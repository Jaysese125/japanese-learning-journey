\## ⛩️ Culture: Gestures when Greeting in Japan

\* \*\*Bowing (お辞儀 - Ojigi):\*\* In Japan, greetings are frequently exchanged while bowing. Depending on the situation and depth of feelings involved, the bow differs in the angle of bending at the waist and length of time for lowering the head.

\* \*\*Family \& Close Friends:\*\* There is no need to bow between family, friends, and people you have a close relationship with.

\* \*\*Physical Contact:\*\* Touching the other person is avoided when greeting—there is no hugging or kissing, even with family and close friends.

\## 🗣️ Key Expressions \& Greetings



\### Time-Based Greetings

| Japanese (Kana) | Romaji | English | Context |

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| \*\*おはようございます\*\* | \*Ohayoo gozaimasu\* | Good morning | Used in the morning (e.g., meeting a neighbour: あさ、きんじょのひとに あいました) |

| \*\*こんにちは\*\* | \*Konnichiwa\* | Hello / Good afternoon | Used during daytime (e.g., meeting an acquaintance: ひる、しりあいに あいました) |

| \*\*こんばんは\*\* | \*Konbanwa\* | Good evening | Used in the evening (e.g., meeting an acquaintance: よる、しりあいに あいました) |



\### Parting \& Workplace Farewells (わかれるとき)

| Japanese (Kana) | Romaji | English | Context |

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| \*\*さようなら\*\* | \*Sayoonara\* | Goodbye | Formal parting (e.g., to a teacher: せんせい、ありがとうございました。さようなら。) |

| \*\*じゃあ、また\*\* | \*Jaa, mata\* | See you / Bye | Casual parting with friends or peers |

| \*\*おさきに しつれいします\*\* | \*Osaki ni shitsureeshimasu\* | Excuse me for leaving first | Leaving work while colleagues are still working |

| \*\*おつかれさまでした\*\* | \*Otsukaresama deshita\* | Thank you for your hard work | Standard workplace response to colleagues |



\### Gratitude, Apologies \& Responses

| Expression | Response (Kana / Romaji) | Context |

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| \*\*ありがとうございます\*\* (\*Arigatoo gozaimasu\*) | \*\*いいえ\*\* (\*Iie\*) | "Thank you very much" → "No, not at all / You're welcome" |

| \*\*すみません\*\* (\*Sumimasen\*) | \*\*いいえ\*\* (\*Iie\*) | "I'm sorry / Excuse me" → "No problem" |

| \*\*すみません\*\* (\*Sumimasen\*) | \*\*はい\*\* (\*Hai\*) | Calling for attention / Asking a question → "Yes?" |

\## 📝 Additional Vocabulary \& Reading Notes

\* \*\*もりさんは さどうを ならっています。もうすぐ おけいこが おわります。\*\*  

&#x20; (\*Mori-san wa sadoo o naratte imasu. Moosugu okeiko ga owarimasu.\*)  

&#x20; \*\*Translation:\*\* Ms. Mori is learning the art of the tea ceremony. The lesson will finish shortly.



\*\*Vocabulary Breakdown:\*\*

\* \*\*さどう (Sadoo):\*\* Tea ceremony

\* \*\*ならっています (Naratte imasu):\*\* Learning / Is learning

\* \*\*おけいこ (Okeiko):\*\* Lesson / Practice
