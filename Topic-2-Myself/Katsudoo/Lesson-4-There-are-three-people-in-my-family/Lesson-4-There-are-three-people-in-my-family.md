## 🎯 Can-Do Objective
**Can-do 4:** Talk about your family.
**Goal:** かぞくの ことを かんたんに はなします (Talk simply about your family)

---

## 📖 Story: Family Picture

**English Summary:**
Talk to your friend while looking at a family picture. Introduce your whole family. Ask and answer questions about family members, their age, where they live, and their work.

**Key Sentence:**
* かぞくの ことを かんたんに はなします。
* *Kazoku no koto o kantan ni hanashimasu.*
* I talk simply about my family.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Vocabulary (My Own Family)

> *In Japanese, when talking to someone else about one's own family, the members of one's family are called differently from the members of other people's families.*

| Japanese | Romaji | English |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ちち | *chichi* | (my) father |
| はは | *haha* | (my) mother |
| あに | *ani* | (my) elder brother |
| あね | *ane* | (my) elder sister |
| おとうと | *otooto* | (my) younger brother |
| いもうと | *imooto* | (my) younger sister |
| おっと | *otto* | (my) husband |
| つま | *tsuma* | (my) wife |
| こども | *kodomo* | (my) child |

## 🔢 Counting People

The number of people is indicated by **"(number) にん (nin)"**.
**1 and 2 are exceptions** and are called **ひとり (hitori)** and **ふたり (futari)**.

| Number | Japanese | Romaji |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | ひとり | *hitori* |
| 2 | ふたり | *futari* |
| 3 | さんにん | *san-nin* |
| 4 | よにん | *yo-nin* |
| 5 | ごにん | *go-nin* |
| 6 | ろくにん | *roku-nin* |
| 7 | しちにん / ななにん | *shichi-nin / nana-nin* |

> **Note:** Be careful with how you read 4 (よん) and 7 (しち/なな).

**Asking about the number of people:**
* なんにんですか。 (*Nan-nin desu ka.*) — How many people are there?

## 🗣️ Example Sentences & Dialogues

### Basic Sentences
| Japanese | Romaji | English |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| わたしの かぞくは さんにんです。 | *Watashi no kazoku wa san-nin desu.* | There are three people in my family. |
| ちちと ははと わたしです。 | *Chichi to haha to watashi desu.* | My father, my mother, and me. |

### Dialogue 1: Asking Mr. Kimura About His Family

| Speaker | Japanese | Romaji | English |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ききて (Interviewer) | きむらさんの ごかぞくは なんにんですか。 | *Kimura-san no gokazoku wa nan-nin desu ka.* | Mr. Kimura, how many people are in your family? |
| きむら (Kimura) | ごにんです。ちちと つま と こども ふたりと わたしです。 | *Go-nin desu. Chichi to tsuma to kodomo futari to watashi desu.* | Five people. My father, my wife, two children, and me. |
| ききて (Interviewer) | おくさんの おしごとは。 | *Okusan no oshigoto wa?* | What is your wife's job? |
| きむら (Kimura) | つまは かいしゃいんです。 | *Tsuma wa kaishain desu.* | My wife is an office worker. |

### Dialogue 2: Asking Dan About His Family

| Speaker | Japanese | Romaji | English |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ききて (Interviewer) | ダンさん、かぞくは なんにんですか。 | *Dan-san, kazoku wa nan-nin desu ka.* | Dan, how many people are in your family? |
| ダン (Dan) | よにん かぞくです。 | *Yo-nin kazoku desu.* | A family of four. |
| ダン (Dan) | ちちは スウェーデンじん、ははは にほんじんです。いもうとも います。 | *Chichi wa Suweeden-jin, haha wa Nihonjin desu. Imooto mo imasu.* | My father is Swedish, my mother is Japanese. I also have a younger sister. |
